China Tech Survival Guide ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at hangxingwujie.github.io (the "Site").

1. Information We Collect

Automatically Collected Information

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect certain information via Google Analytics 4, including:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or search engine
  • Browser type, device type, and operating system
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level only — never precise)
  • Language settings

This data is anonymized and aggregated. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or IP addresses through our analytics.

Information You Voluntarily Provide

We currently do not offer comment sections, contact forms, newsletter signups, or user accounts on the Site. Therefore, we do not collect any information you actively submit. If we add such features in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

2. Cookies

The Site uses the following types of cookies:

  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4): These track aggregated usage statistics. GA4 uses first-party cookies set by our domain. No personal data is stored. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Language preference (localStorage): When you switch the Site's language using our language selector, we store your preference in your browser's localStorage. This is not a cookie and does not involve any server-side storage.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. Note that disabling analytics cookies does not affect your ability to use the Site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Understand which articles are most helpful to travelers
  • Identify which countries our visitors come from
  • Improve the Site's content and navigation
  • Monitor Site performance and fix technical issues

We do not use your data for advertising targeting, profiling, or automated decision-making.

4. Third-Party Services

The Site uses the following third-party services that may process data:

  • Google Analytics: Privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Translate: When you use our language selector, the page content is translated via Google's translation widget. Google may process the text content of the page. Privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • GitHub Pages: Our Site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub's privacy policy is at docs.github.com

Affiliate partners may place cookies to track the referral. These cookies are set by the respective affiliate networks, not by us. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months by default. Language preference in localStorage persists until you clear your browser data. We do not maintain any separate databases or servers storing visitor data.

Depending on your jurisdiction (GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA in California, etc.), you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing. Since we do not collect personal data directly, exercising these rights primarily involves:

The Site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Site, please contact us.
